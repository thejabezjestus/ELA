ABOUT US

The name Elysium comes from the Elysian Fields, the ancient Greek ideal of a place where people live in peace and security. That is what we aim to give our clients: clear, dependable legal counsel that lets you move forward with confidence. Elysium Legal Advisors is a full-service law firm advising businesses, families and individuals. Whether you are forming a company, buying a property, planning your estate or facing a dispute, we take the time to understand your situation and explain your options in plain language. You will always know where your matter stands and what comes next.

Our work spans corporate and business law, family and personal law, property and real estate law, and dispute resolution and litigation. Each matter is handled with careful preparation, attention to detail and respect for your goals, whether the best result comes through negotiation or in court.
